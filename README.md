# Yuhao Huai

### Robotics · Mechatronics · Mechanical Design

I work on robotic perception, wearable sensing, and electromechanical systems. My projects connect mechanical design, embedded control, sensor integration, and experimental validation.

My background is in mechanical engineering, with research and project experience at Stanford University and the University of Wisconsin–Madison. This portfolio brings together my research, course projects, and engineering contributions. Each project page describes the problem, my contribution, the technical approach, and the materials currently available.

# Tendon-Driven Bionic Hand

An anatomy-informed robotic hand project exploring printable finger structures and tendon actuation using servos and nylon cords.

**Area:** Robotic hardware  
**Stage:** Single-finger prototype; full-hand integration in progress  
**Tools and methods:** Mechanical design · 3D printing · Tendon routing · Servo actuation

## Context

Ongoing hardware development documented in local CAD records and project notes.

## My contribution

- Iterated finger designs from ligament-inspired structures through four- and six-tendon layouts to nylon-cord actuation.
- Built a little-finger mechanical prototype with one cord driven by one servo, as recorded in the project notes.
- Developed spool geometry and whole-hand design iterations, with versioned manufacturing files.

## Technical approach

The design starts from bone geometry, joint axes, ligament attachment, and tendon travel. Development proceeds through CAD revisions, printable geometry, assembly, and planned measurements of range of motion and actuation behavior.

## Available materials and validation

Local files include CAD iterations and manufacturing exports. Full-hand control and electronics integration remain in progress. Quantitative results for backlash, load, repeatability, and durability have not yet been recorded in the reviewed index.

[Related servo-array test device — private; access required](https://github.com/11HYH11/Bionic-Hand-Test-Device)

The test-device repository is a related component, not a complete release of the hand. MyoHand/myo_sim, Aero Hand, and ORCA Hand materials in the working directory are third-party references and are not presented as original implementations.

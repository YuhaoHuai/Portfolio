# Interactive Mohr’s Circle Learning Tool

A physical and computational teaching tool for visualizing stress states and three-dimensional Mohr’s circle transformations.

**Area:** Engineering education  
**Stage:** Research project; conference publication  
**Tools and methods:** MATLAB · Mathematical modeling · Physical interfaces · Signal processing

## Context

Detlor Research Group, University of Wisconsin–Madison, May 2023–June 2024.

## My contribution

- Designed and fabricated teaching tools for mechanics-of-materials instruction.
- Worked on mathematical modeling and MATLAB simulation for stress transformation and visualization.
- Assisted with a converter that translates mechanical inputs into electrical signals for interactive simulation.

## Technical approach

A physical interface connects student manipulation to a computational visualization of stress transformations.

## Available materials and validation

A co-authored paper was published at the 2024 ASEE Annual Conference & Exposition. Source code and fabrication files have not yet been added to this portfolio.

Publication: Livingston-Jha, S., Deng, H., Huai, Y., & Detlor, J. (2024). *Learning Tool to Enhance Understanding of Stress States and Mohr’s Circle*. ASEE Annual Conference & Exposition. [ASEE paper](https://peer.asee.org/learning-tool-to-enhance-understanding-of-stress-states-and-mohr-s-circle.pdf). DOI: 10.18260/1-2--47723.
